How it all started

A bigger table,
the same love.

For as long as I can remember, there was always something in the oven. Birthdays, holidays, family dinners, last-minute gatherings. Whatever the occasion, I showed up with something homemade. Not because anyone asked me to. Because food was always how I shared my love with people.

Sweet & Sour is that same love, now available to order. Every pastry, every treat, every finger food is still made by hand, in my home kitchen, with the same care I’ve always put into everything I make.

I’m just baking for a bigger table now, and I couldn’t be happier about it.
