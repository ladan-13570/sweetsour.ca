The Yummy Studio Story

Where Sweet Comfort Meets a Bold Twist.

At Yummy Studio, we believe in simple, honest ingredients and the art of the perfect bake. Our story is built on the balance of two worlds:

The Sweet Icons
It starts with the classics. From our signature Trio-Leches and rich Chocolate Fudge Cake to the fragrant Baklava Cake, we create treats designed to melt in your mouth. Whether it’s a nostalgic Date Cookie or a modern Caramel & Nescafe treat, or our timeless classic chocolate chip, we bake for the soul.

The Bold Twist
But we don’t stop at sweet. For the adventurous and the young at heart, we created the Signature Sour Collection. We’ve reinvented snacking by blending the zesty kick of plums, barberries, and cherries into a perfect \"sour-sweet\" fusion.

The Artisanal Finish
From our handcrafted cakes to our traditional Vegetable Pickles, every product is made by hand with no shortcuts. At Yummy Studio, we create the bold, beautiful flavours you’ll remember.
