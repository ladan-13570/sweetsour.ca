Our Story

Two sides,
one kitchen

01

The Sweet Side

It started with a simple chocolate chip cookie and a dream. Today, our kitchen turns out classics like Trio-Leches, Chocolate Fudge Cake, and fragrant Baklava Cake — recipes perfected over years, designed to make you feel at home.

02

The Sour Twist

Not everyone craves sugar. For the bold and the curious, we created our Signature Sour Collection — a zesty fusion of plums, barberries, and cherries that reinvents snacking. Think tangy, think addictive, think unforgettable.

03

The Handmade Promise

No factories, no shortcuts, no preservatives. Every cookie is scooped by hand, every cake is layered with care, and every jar of pickles follows a family recipe. That’s the Sweet & Sour difference.
