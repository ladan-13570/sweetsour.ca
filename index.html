Our Story

Where Sweet Comfort Meets a Bold Twist

🍪

The Sweet Side

It starts with the classics. From our signature Trio-Leches and rich Chocolate Fudge Cake to the fragrant Baklava Cake, we create treats designed to melt in your mouth. Whether it’s a nostalgic Date Cookie or a modern Caramel & Nescafe treat, we bake for the soul.

🍋

The Bold Twist

We don’t stop at sweet. For the adventurous and the young at heart, we created the Signature Sour Collection — blending the zesty kick of plums, barberries, and cherries into a perfect “sour-sweet” fusion you won’t forget.

✨

The Artisanal Finish

From handcrafted cakes to traditional Vegetable Pickles, every product is made by hand with no shortcuts. At Sweet & Sour Treats, we create the bold, beautiful flavours you’ll remember.
